## The context

**A portfolio should do more than list what I can do.**

My previous portfolio introduced me as a developer. Since then, the way I work has become clearer: I care about the experience behind an interface as much as the code that brings it to life. I built rahim.design to reflect that broader practice and give people a direct sense of how I think, design and build.

## The challenge

**The challenge: make a broad skill set feel like one clear offer.**

UX/UI design and front-end development can easily read as two separate services. The challenge was to show how they reinforce each other, while helping a potential client quickly understand what I can contribute, see relevant work and find a straightforward way to start a conversation.

## The approach

**One clear story, from first impression to first conversation.**

The portfolio brings my introduction, selected projects, services and contact information into one connected experience. Each section answers a practical question: who I am, how I approach a project, what I can help with, and where to go next. The aim is to make the work speak for itself while keeping the path to getting in touch easy to find.

## Important decisions

Some decisions that shape the experience.

- **Show the connection between design and code.** The portfolio presents both as parts of the same process, from understanding the experience to implementing the interface.
- **Let real work carry the message.** Project pages give visitors concrete examples instead of relying on claims alone.
- **Keep the next step visible.** Services and contact information help turn interest into a conversation.
- **Use motion with purpose.** GSAP adds movement where it supports the experience and reinforces the site's character.

## Development

**The experience had to hold together in the browser, not just in a design file.**

I built the portfolio with React, TypeScript and Vite, using GSAP for animation. Building the site myself let me carry decisions from UX and visual design through to the working interface, while shaping the content and interactions around the people I want to work with.

The live portfolio is available at [rahim-dev-me.vercel.app](https://rahim-dev-me.vercel.app/).
