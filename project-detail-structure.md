## The context

**It all starts with a problem.**

Users do not necessarily lack choice. They often lack an environment that helps them find the right product without noise.

The objective was therefore to create an experience that made product discovery simpler, clearer and above all less intimidating.

---

## The challenge

**The challenge: keep it simple without making the experience simplistic.**

- **Too much information** — The user should be able to understand their status and next actions in seconds.
- **Too much friction** — Completing a purchase had to feel almost immediate.
- **An experience to be made trustworthy** — The interface had to reassure the user without becoming intrusive.

---

## The approach

**So how to solve this?**

I chose to build the experience around a simple idea: one important action at a time.

Instead of multiplying visible information simultaneously, the interface gradually gives the user what they need.
